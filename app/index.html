About

Vice Coast Cover Studio turns your photos into a multi-panel Vice Coast poster: pick a layout, a sunset grade, write the caption, export a PNG for your feed. Fonts: Anton & Pacifico, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see app/fonts/OFL.txt). All 9 sample photos were made with an image model — no real people, no game artwork.
